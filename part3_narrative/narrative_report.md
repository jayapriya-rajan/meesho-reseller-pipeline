**Context:** This update compares Ethnic Wear revenue in May 2026 with April 2026

**Insight:** Ethnic Wear revenue rose from INR 104520.77 in April to INR 185107.61 in May. That is a month-on-month growth of 77.1%. It is above the 8% alert threshold, so the category is flagged.

**Implication:** The Ethnic Wear category manager should check what ran on Ethnic Wear in May, such as a sale, an offer or a new listing push. **Implication:** The regional manager should ask the category manager whether the May promotion ended and whether any popular Ethnic Wear items ran out of stock in June. Next check: the category manager should compare June with April's level of INR 104520.77 to see whether June is a drop below the usual level, not just a return to it. The regional manager should wait for this check before cutting Ethnic Wear targets.
